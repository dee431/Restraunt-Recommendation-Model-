🍔 Model Overview
A smart, content‑based restaurant recommender that learns your taste – not from past orders, but from what you tell it! 🧠✨

⚙️ How it works

🧬 Cuisine DNA – Every restaurant is encoded with a multi‑hot vector of its cuisines (e.g., Italian 🍝, Sushi 🍣, Vegan 🌱).
💸 Price & Rating – Numerical preferences (cost 💰, price tier 📊, rating ⭐) are standardized and blended with cuisine signals.
🔍 Similarity Engine – Using cosine similarity, the model finds the closest matches to your ideal dining profile – whether you crave Japanese in Bangalore 🇯🇵🌆 or a budget‑friendly pizza in New Delhi 🍕🏙️.
📋 What you get

📝 A ranked list of restaurants with similarity scores,
🎛️ An interactive dashboard (dropdowns, sliders, click‑to‑search),
📊 Visual insights (rating bar charts 📈, cuisine pie charts 🥧) – all in one Colab‑friendly interface.
💡 Why it’s creative

🤝 Combines explicit preferences with real‑world restaurant data,
🌍 Adapts instantly to any city or cuisine mix,
🚀 No history required – pure content‑based discovery,
🎨 And it’s fully visual, turning data into delicious decisions!
🍽️ Perfect for food lovers 😋, trip planners ✈️, or anyone who wants to skip the scroll and find their next great meal 🥘🍷🥂
